## 🎮 How to Navigate the 3D City

### Camera Controls:

- **Left Click + Drag**: Rotate view
- **Right Click + Drag**: Pan camera
- **Scroll Wheel**: Zoom in/out
- **Mouse Hover**: Highlight building (shows glow)
- **Click Building**: Navigate to that feature

### Finding Token Buildings:

**Quick Find - Token Vault:**

1. Zoom out to see full city
2. Look for the **GOLDEN/YELLOW tower**
3. It's in the **southwest quadrant** (bottom-left from center)
4. Icon: 🔒
5. Click to access minting (owner only)

**Quick Find - Token Faucet:**

1. First locate Token Vault (golden tower)
2. Look **slightly diagonal** from it (towards outer edge)
3. Find the **TEAL/TURQUOISE building** nearby
4. Icon: 💧
5. Click to claim free tokens!

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Can't find buildings?**

   - Zoom out (scroll back)
   - Rotate view slowly in a circle
   - Look for the color: Teal (faucet) or Gold (vault)

2. **Buildings too small?**

   - Zoom in (scroll forward)
   - Buildings will appear larger and easier to click

3. **Wrong building?**

   - Each building shows name on hover
   - Wait for tooltip to appear before clicking

4. **3D too slow?**
   - Use sidebar or direct URL instead
   - 3D is optional, all features accessible via links
